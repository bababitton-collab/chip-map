Scoring and verification

Dated questions

The map isThe maps are paired with {{questions}} dated questions across the chain — questions tied to upcoming reports, policy decisions and industry events. Each comes with pre-written yes/no criteria, and each scored question names an impact basket: the companies its answer bears on. Observation-only questions ({{observe_only}} today) carry no basket and are not scored.

Scored forward against the market

When a yes or no answer lands, the basket is measured from the close of the next trading session against an equal-weight benchmark of every priced company on the map (EW_MAP), with the PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (SOX), tracked through {{sox}}, that map's own second benchmark — named on its card above — shown alongside it. The official score is the basket's excess over EW_MAP at a primary horizon of {{primary_horizon}} trading sessions; {{diagnostic_horizons}} sessions are kept as diagnostics. Mixed or unclear answers are recorded as observations and are not scored.

Pre-registered and verifiable

Each question's scoring contract — its baskets, yes/no criteria, horizons and benchmarks — is hashed with SHA-256 and published, with the date it was committed, in commitments.json. {{n_valid}} of the {{n_committed}} questions committed so far were committed before their answer date, and commitments.json marks any that were not. Once a question resolves, its full card is published free in track_public.json with the exact contract text, and a Verify button on the Track Record recomputes the hash in your browser and compares it with the published commitment.

Forward testing, no backtests

Nothing here is backtested. Every forecast is scored forward from the day its question is marked, and the record — resolved questions, and the hit rate and excess returns with their sample size — is on the Track Record.

How are forecasts registered?

The scoring contract is hashed and dated in commitments.json. When a question resolves, its exact contract is published with the result. Verify recomputes the hash in your browser. Commitments made on or after the answer date are marked invalid as preregistrations.

What's free, and what needs the key?

The map, every chokepoint, the calendar, and every resolved question with its verification are public. Upcoming questions and their baskets unlock with a free key sent by email. Subscribe, confirm the first email, and the key arrives in the welcome email.
