Measurement method

Dated questions

The map isThe maps are paired with {{questions}} dated questions across the chain. Each has a fixed answer date, a written YES/NO rule and a registered impact basket: the companies its answer bears on. Observation-only questions ({{observe_only}} today) carry no basket and are not scored.

Forward measurement

After an answer is marked, the registered basket is measured from the close of the next trading session. The official result is excess return versus the equal-weight map (EW_MAP) at the registered primary horizon of {{primary_horizon}} trading sessions; {{diagnostic_horizons}} sessions are kept as diagnostics, alongside the PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (SOX), tracked through {{sox}} each map's own second benchmark, named on its card above. Mixed or unclear answers are recorded as observations and are not scored.

Public commitments

The complete scoring contract — baskets, YES/NO criteria, horizons and benchmarks — is hashed with SHA-256 and published with the date it was committed, in commitments.json. {{n_valid}} of the {{n_committed}} contracts committed so far were committed before their answer date. {{prereg_rule}} Once a question resolves, its full card is published in track_public.json with the exact contract text, and Verify recomputes the hash in your browser.

Forward testing, no backtests

Questions enter the record before outcomes and subsequent price moves are known. Nothing here is backtested: every published result is measured forward from the day its question is marked, and shown with its sample size on the Track record.

What is Linchpin Signal?

{{brand}} maps physical supply chains, identifies concentrated dependencies and publishes dated questions whose outcomes are measured forward against the market — {{companies}} companies in {{n_layers}} layers, with {{chokepoints}} {{chokepoint_kind}} chokepoints marked, paired with {{questions}} dated questions — {{n_maps}} maps covering {{companies}} companies, with {{chokepoints}} chokepoints marked, paired with {{questions}} dated questions.

What is public?

Every map, chokepoint, event date and resolved question with its verification is public, and so is every measured result. Upcoming question details and registered baskets require the free weekly key. Subscribe, confirm the first email, and the key arrives in the welcome email.

How are questions registered?

Each scoring contract is hashed with SHA-256 and dated in commitments.json. When the question resolves, the full contract is published and Verify recomputes the hash in your browser. Late commitments are labeled and never counted as preregistered.

Is this investment advice?

No. {{brand}} publishes supply-chain research, questions and measurements. It does not publish recommendations.
