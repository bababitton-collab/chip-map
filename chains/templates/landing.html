How it works: a semiconductor supply chain map with dated questions

The chain, in layers

{{brand}} maps the physical AI supply chain in {{n_layers}} layers: {{layers}}. It covers {{companies}} listed companies and who supplies whom among them — the AI infrastructure behind the models, from raw materials to data centers and the power that runs them.

Semiconductor chokepoints

{{chokepoints}} chokepoints are marked on the map: highly concentrated dependencies with few viable alternatives, such as {{chokepoint_examples}}. Where a listed challenger exists, a pulse compares the holder's 13-week return with the challengers'.

Dated questions

The map is paired with {{questions}} dated questions across the chain — questions tied to upcoming reports, policy decisions and industry events. Each comes with pre-written yes/no criteria, and each scored question names an impact basket: the companies its answer bears on. Observation-only questions ({{observe_only}} today) carry no basket and are not scored.

Scored forward against the market

When a yes or no answer lands, the basket is measured from the close of the next trading session against an equal-weight benchmark of every priced company on the map (EW_MAP), with the PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (SOX), tracked through {{sox}}, shown alongside it. The official score is the basket's excess over EW_MAP at a primary horizon of {{primary_horizon}} trading sessions; {{diagnostic_horizons}} sessions are kept as diagnostics. Mixed or unclear answers are recorded as observations and are not scored.

Pre-registered and verifiable

Each question's scoring contract — its baskets, yes/no criteria, horizons and benchmarks — is hashed with SHA-256 and published, with the date it was committed, in commitments.json. {{n_valid}} of the {{n_committed}} questions committed so far were committed before their answer date, and commitments.json marks any that were not. Once a question resolves, its full card is published free in track_public.json with the exact contract text, and a Verify button on the Track Record recomputes the hash in your browser and compares it with the published commitment.

Forward testing, no backtests

Nothing here is backtested. Every forecast is scored forward from the day its question is marked, and the record — resolved questions, and the hit rate and excess returns with their sample size — is on the Track Record.

Questions

What is Linchpin Signal?

{{brand}} is a map of the physical supply chain behind AI — {{companies}} companies in {{n_layers}} layers, with {{chokepoints}} {{chokepoint_kind}} chokepoints marked — paired with {{questions}} dated questions whose answers are measured forward against the market and published once they resolve.

How are forecasts registered?

Each question's scoring contract is serialised canonically and hashed with SHA-256, and the hash is published with its commit date in commitments.json. The build stops if a committed contract no longer matches its hash. When a question resolves, the exact contract text is published with its card, and the Verify button recomputes the SHA-256 in your browser with the Web Crypto API and compares it with the published hash. A commitment made on or after the answer date is marked as not a valid preregistration.
