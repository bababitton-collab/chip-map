How it is scored

A question is scored from the close of the first session after its answer is marked. The official measure is the registered basket's excess over an equal-weight benchmark of every priced company on that map, at the primary horizon. Shorter and longer horizons, and each map's second benchmark, are kept as diagnostics and never as a second chance. Mixed or unclear answers are recorded as observations and are not scored.

Nothing here is backtested. Every number is measured forward from a mark, against a contract published before the answer date.
