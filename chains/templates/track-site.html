How results are measured

A question is measured from the first market close after its answer is marked. The official result is the registered basket's excess return versus the equal-weight map at the registered primary horizon.

Other horizons and each map's second benchmark remain diagnostic. Partial answers are preserved as observations and are not scored. Every result is shown with its sample size.

A contract counts as preregistered only when it was committed before the answer date. Late commitments remain public and are excluded from preregistered results. No results are backtested.
